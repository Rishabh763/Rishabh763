🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm an M.Tech student at IIT Bombay in the Centre of Studies in Resources Engineering (CSRE), specializing in Geoinformatics and Natural Resources Engineering (graduating 2027). Before that I did my B.Tech in Computer Science at D.J. Sanghvi College of Engineering, Mumbai.

My work sits where three things meet: machine learning, computer vision on satellite and planetary imagery, and building things for the web.

🔭 Currently working on: my thesis, Europa Surface Evolution, under Prof. Deepak Singh, processing NASA Galileo SSI imagery and NIMS hyperspectral data to study how Europa's icy surface fractures and resurfaces
🌱 Currently learning: generative models (GANs and their variants), vision-language models, and sharpening DSA
🏆 GATE DA AIR 790
🎓 Class Representative for my M.Tech batch and member of the departmental council
💼 Open to SDE and ML roles
⚡ Fun fact: I spend my days counting cracks on a moon 600 million km away
